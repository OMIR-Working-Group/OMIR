3D Field Map

A spatial view of how OMIR's fields relate to its fieldsets. The outer ring holds the five resources plus the Bundle envelope — each is a fieldset. Their fields orbit them. The bright cluster in the centre holds the shared fieldsets — the common $defs (Meta, Confidence, Decay, Provenance, Reference, Extension, Instant, UnitInterval, Id) that many resources reuse. The web of lines from the ring into the centre is the interoperability story: independent resources built from the same shared parts.

Drag to rotate · scroll to zoom · click a resource (or a chip below) to isolate its fields · hover a node for detail. Everything runs in your browser — no libraries, no network. Derived directly from the R1 JSON Schemas.
